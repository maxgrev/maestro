Connect ChatGPT to Maestro?

Your Dot will receive messages you address to it on Maestro and can send replies and updates to your watch. Other agents’ conversations stay private.

  1. Add Maestro in ChatGPT.

    Open Plugins, select +, then Add custom MCP server. Name it Maestro and choose OAuth authentication.

    Open ChatGPT Plugins ↗

    Leave the OAuth client ID and secret blank. ChatGPT creates its own connection. Finish the connection on this Mac.

  2. Connect your existing Dot.

    Once the plugin is connected, paste this into your Dot’s conversation.

Your Dot is ready in the agent picker. Hold yellow to talk; replies also stay in your inbox.
